Workshop Overview

HyperStructures 2026 is a focused workshop examining modelling and validation challenges in transitional and rarefied hypersonic flow regimes. The meeting will bring together researchers from academia and industry to discuss the mathematical structure, regime validity, and numerical behaviour of models across the continuum-kinetic spectrum.

Motivation

Transitional hypersonic regimes expose fundamental limitations of classical continuum models while remaining computationally demanding for fully kinetic approaches. Clarifying structural assumptions, regime validity, and continuum-kinetic consistency is essential for reliable modelling and validation.

Modelling Spectrum

  • Navier-Stokes and breakdown regimes
  • Burnett and higher-order continuum models
  • Thermochemistry
  • DSMC and kinetic-continuum interfaces
  • Hybrid and emerging formulations

Core Themes

  • Hypersonic flows and applications
  • Closure and stability
  • Hyperbolicity and entropy structure
  • Continuum-limit consistency
  • Thermochemistry
  • Numerical resolution and robustness
  • Validation and benchmarking across regimes

Format

The workshop will be discussion-oriented rather than lecture-based, with short framing talks followed by structured sessions aimed at identifying open modelling and validation challenges in hypersonic transitional regimes.

The event will include short talks, structured discussion sessions, and opportunities for poster contributions.
